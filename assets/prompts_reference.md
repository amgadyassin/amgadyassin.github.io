## 1. Portfolio Hero Portrait (Executive Tech Leader)
* **Target Location:** Portfolio Website Hero Section (`#hero .hero-image`)
* **Aspect Ratio:** `3:4` or `4:5` (Vertical Portrait)
* **Framing:** Waist-up or Chest-up portrait, slight 15-degree turn, facing camera.

### Prompt:
```text
A professional executive corporate portrait of the man in the reference images. He is a Senior Data Analyst and enterprise technology leader with short dark hair, warm brown eyes, clean-shaven with faint groomed stubble, and a confident, approachable, friendly smile. Wearing a tailored modern charcoal grey or dark navy blazer with a crisp white collared dress shirt (open collar, no tie). Professional corporate studio lighting with soft directional key light, subtle rim light highlighting shoulders. Minimalist blurred modern corporate tech office background with deep slate tones and soft natural depth-of-field. High-end editorial corporate photography, 85mm f/1.4 lens, razor-sharp focus on eyes, ultra-realistic skin texture, natural studio lighting, 8k resolution, cinematic professional quality.
```

## 2. LinkedIn Profile Headshot (1:1 Circular Crop)
* **Target Location:** LinkedIn Profile Photo
* **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (Square, centered)
* **Framing:** Close-up head and shoulders, centered composition so it fits a circular mask without clipping.

### Prompt:
```text
Executive LinkedIn profile headshot of the man in the reference images, with 1:1 square centered framing perfectly optimized for a circular crop. He is a Senior Data Analyst and Business Intelligence leader with short neat dark hair, warm expressive eyes with natural studio catchlights, clean-shaven with light stubble, and an engaging, confident, warm smile. Wearing a premium tailored dark suit jacket with a crisp white dress shirt. Clean, seamless studio background with a gentle gradient of deep navy to dark slate. Premium commercial photography lighting, soft natural shadows, pristine razor sharpness, lifelike skin tone and texture, hyper-realistic, 8k resolution.
```

## 3. Alternative Modern Casual Tech Look
* **Target Location:** Secondary profile / Speaking / Bio
* **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` or `3:4`

### Prompt:
```text
Modern corporate tech portrait of a male data analytics expert matching the reference photos. Wearing a smart-casual tailored textured grey sports coat over a white crew-neck or open-collar shirt. Relaxed yet commanding professional posture, authentic smile, eyes sharp and clear. Natural architectural interior background with soft blurred daylight and contemporary warm wood and glass elements. Editorial style, Leica 50mm f/1.2 look, lifelike skin pores and details, true-to-life colors.
```
